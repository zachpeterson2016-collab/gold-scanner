Gold (XAUUSD) — How the $4k trade actually works

Visual cheat-sheet: market structure → liquidity → setup → position sizing → sessions → prop-firm survival. Gold ≈ $4,140/oz today.

1Market structure — the only thing that matters first

Price moves in swings. Label every swing high and low. The relationship between them tells you who is in control and which direction you're allowed to trade.

HH Higher High  ·  HL Higher Low → uptrend
LH Lower High  ·  LL Lower Low → downtrend
Rule #1: you only take trades in the direction of the structure. In an uptrend you buy HLs; you never short a HH just because "it's high".

2BOS vs CHoCH — "is the trend continuing or flipping?"

BOS (Break of Structure) = price breaks the previous high in an uptrend → trend confirmed, keep buying dips. CHoCH (Change of Character) = price breaks the last higher low → first warning the trend is over.

Green dashed = BOS — continuation. Each one is permission to look for the next long.
Orange dashed = CHoCH — the last HL gets taken out. Stop buying. Wait for a LH, then look for shorts.
Beginner trap: the first red candle after a HH is not a CHoCH. It only counts when price closes below the most recent higher low.

3Liquidity sweep — why gold "stop-hunts" you, and how to use it

Gold loves to spike through obvious levels, trigger everyone's stop-losses, then reverse. Once you can see where the stops are, the spike becomes your entry instead of your loss.

Where are the stops? Below equal lows, above equal highs, below obvious trendlines, and at round numbers ($4,100, $4,150). Mark them before the session starts.

4The setup your buddy trades — trend pullback into demand, 1:4 reward

Put diagrams 1–3 together. HTF uptrend → price pulls back into the zone the last impulse launched from → you buy with a tight stop and target the previous high. Real gold numbers below.

Why the zone works: it's where big buyers filled last time and left unfilled orders. Price returning there tends to bounce.
Why 1:4 works: you can lose 3 of 4 trades and still break even. Your buddy isn't right more often — he's paid more when he's right.

4bThe entry trigger — zoom to 15m inside the zone

You don't buy just because price touched the zone. You wait for the 15m chart to flip: it was making LH/LL on the way down, then it breaks a lower high (CHoCH) and confirms (BOS). That's the trigger.

5The $4k math — same trade on a $25k, $50k and $100k account

1 lot of gold = 100 oz, so every $1 move = $100 per lot. Your buddy's $4k is a $100k-account number. The identical trade on your $25k challenge pays $1,000 — and that is correct, not small.

Lot formula to memorise: Lots = (Account × Risk%) ÷ (Stop in $ × 100). On $25k with a $10 stop that's 0.25 lot. With a tight $5 stop from the 15m trigger it's 0.50 lot — same $250 risk, double the payout per $1.
